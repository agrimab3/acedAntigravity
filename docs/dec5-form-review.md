**Explanation:** In a 30°-60°-90° triangle with hypotenuse 12, BC = 6 and AC = 6√3. Angle ABC = 60°, so BD splits it into two 30° angles. In right triangle BCD, tan 30° = CD/BC, so CD = 6/√3 = 2√3.
