# Aced Mock Test Rules

**Effective Date:** October 14, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 14, 2026

These Rules govern Aced's unofficial timed ACT-style mock examinations and supplement the Terms of Service and Refund and Payment Policy.

## 1. Eligibility
Participants must be at least 13, have an eligible Aced account, and satisfy any permission required by law. Registration is subject to capacity.

## 2. Confirmed Examination
The currently configured mock test is **Saturday, December 5, 2026**, ahead of the December 12 ACT. The planned fee is **$2.00 USD**. Other dates may be announced after they are fully configured; a displayed next-test label is not confirmation that registration is open.

## 3. Time Zone and Start
Students select a supported US time zone at registration, which is locked for that registration. They must start on December 5 in that time zone, no later than **9:00 PM local time**. An existing session may continue past midnight according to its server-backed section deadlines.

## 4. Sections
| Section | Questions | Time |
|---|---:|---:|
| English | 50 | 35 minutes |
| Math | 45 | 50 minutes |
| Reading | 36 | 40 minutes |
| Science | 40 | 40 minutes |
| **Total** | **171** | **165 minutes** |

Sections proceed in this order: English, Math, Reading, Science. The total excludes breaks and transitions.

## 5. Transitions and Required Break
A five-second transition follows English and Reading. A **mandatory 10-minute break** follows Math. Students cannot skip the required break in the deployed public test flow. Section deadlines are enforced by the server.

## 6. Connection Interruptions and Saving
If disconnected, students may sign back in to resume an existing session using the same registered account. Timers do not pause. Answers can be saved locally while offline and may synchronize if selected before the applicable deadline and received within the **15-minute offline-sync window**. A normal save grace period of five seconds also exists. Recovery is not guaranteed in every circumstance. Do not clear browser storage during an active exam.

## 7. Calculator and Conduct
Aced's built-in calculator is available in the Math section only. The interface also indicates that ACT-approved calculators may be used. Students should work independently and not interfere with exam systems or other participants. The AI tutor and immediate correct-answer feedback are unavailable during active testing.

## 8. Results
Results for the December 5 mock test are scheduled for **Sunday, December 6, 2026 at 2:00 PM Pacific Time**. Results and explanations are not immediately revealed during the test. Mock scores are unofficial and do not guarantee performance on an official ACT.

## 9. Accessibility
Aced has some accessibility-oriented interface features, but has not verified full conformance or established a formal extended-time accommodation workflow. Students needing support should contact support@aceditprep.com before purchasing or starting. Aced will explain available options and review requests as required by law. **[REVIEW: Accessibility and accommodations procedure before paid launch.]**

## 10. Technical Problems, Changes, and Cancellation
Report issues to support@aceditprep.com without sending passwords or card information. Material changes, cancellations, and failures to provide paid access will be handled under the Refund and Payment Policy and applicable law.
