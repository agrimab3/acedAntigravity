# Aced Refund and Payment Policy

**Effective Date:** October 14, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 14, 2026

This Policy explains payments and refunds for Aced's optional paid mock examinations. Regular ACT practice and AI tutoring are currently free.

## 1. Price
The planned mock-test fee is **$2.00 USD per registration**. This is a one-time payment, not a subscription. The final total and any applicable additional charges will be disclosed before checkout.

## 2. Payment Processing
When live checkout is enabled, Stripe processes payments through hosted Checkout. Aced does not directly collect full card numbers or card security codes. A registration is marked paid only after verified payment confirmation, not merely because a browser reaches a success page. Stripe is intentionally in test mode at the time these drafts were prepared.

## 3. General Refund Rule
Mock-test purchases are generally non-refundable because they reserve limited scheduled access, subject to applicable law and the exceptions below. Students must acknowledge the applicable refund conditions before checkout.

## 4. Seat Unavailable
If payment succeeds but Aced cannot provide a seat because capacity is exhausted, the current system is designed to initiate an automatic refund and queue a notification email. Stripe and the payment institution determine when refunded funds appear.

## 5. Incorrect Charges and Service Problems
For duplicate or incorrect charges, missing paid access, cancellation by Aced, or other problems, contact support@aceditprep.com. We will investigate and provide legally required remedies or other appropriate resolutions. There is currently no general self-service refund button. Exceptions may require authorized action in Stripe Dashboard.

## 6. Missed Tests
Missing the exam, starting too late, or voluntarily declining to participate does not ordinarily entitle a student to an automatic refund. This does not waive mandatory consumer rights or remedies for Aced's failure to provide the purchased service.

## 7. Failed or Delayed Payments
Abandoned or failed Checkout does not create a paid registration. Payment confirmation can be delayed while Stripe webhooks are processed. Contact support if a charge appears without confirmed registration before attempting another payment.

## 8. Disputes and Changes
Students retain applicable payment dispute rights. Policy updates will not retroactively remove rights applicable to an existing purchase.
