# Aced Accessibility Statement

**Effective Date:** October 14, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 14, 2026

Aced believes ACT preparation should be accessible to students with different needs and abilities. We are committed to improving the accessibility and usability of our website, practice tools, AI tutoring, and mock examinations.

## 1. Our Approach
Aced includes semantic interface controls, labels, live regions, radio inputs, timer labels, keyboard-accessible calculator controls, and reduced-motion CSS. These features support accessibility, but do not establish that all experiences are accessible to every user.

## 2. Standards
We use principles from the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) to guide improvements. Aced has not completed a verified platform-wide conformance assessment and does not claim WCAG certification or a particular conformance level.

## 3. Areas Requiring Further Testing
Timed examinations, mathematical notation, figures, graphs, interactive calculators, keyboard navigation across complex flows, and screen-reader behavior require further evaluation. This is not a verified list of all existing barriers.

## 4. Mock-Test Accommodations
The current mock-test implementation has no verified formal extended-time setting or established accommodations workflow. Students who need accessibility-related support should contact support@aceditprep.com before purchasing or starting. We will explain available options and consider reasonable requests as required by law. We do not claim to reproduce every official ACT accommodation.

## 5. Reporting Accessibility Problems
Email support@aceditprep.com with the affected page or feature, the difficulty encountered, and any relevant browser or assistive-technology details. Please do not include passwords or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

## 6. Responses and Improvements
We review accessibility feedback and work toward reasonable improvements or alternatives where feasible and required by applicable law. Some issues may take time to resolve.
