# Aced Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 14, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 14, 2026

Your privacy is important to Aced ("Aced," "we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our website, ACT preparation tools, AI tutoring features, and mock examinations.

We believe students should understand what happens to their information. This Policy also explains when we share information with trusted service providers, how we use data to personalize your learning experience, and what choices and privacy rights you have.

## 1. Audience and Age
Aced is intended for people aged 13 and older. We request an affirmation of age eligibility without collecting a full date of birth for that purpose. We do not knowingly permit users under 13 to register. Contact support@aceditprep.com if you believe someone under 13 has registered.

## 2. Information Collected
**Accounts and consent:** Google sign-in information such as name, email, account identifier, and available profile information; age affirmation; Terms version and acceptance time; account preferences.

**Learning:** Practice answers, question attempts, scores, progress, topic mastery, difficulty recommendations, mock-test registrations, section attempts, and results.

**AI tutor:** The student's message and relevant question text, passage, answer choices, section, topic, difficulty, prior hints, and learning-performance indicators. Aced currently stores up to three earlier tutor replies in the relevant practice state. Please do not include unnecessary sensitive personal information in tutor messages.

**Payments:** When paid checkout is enabled, Stripe processes payments. Aced may retain Checkout Session, PaymentIntent, refund, and registration identifiers and status. Aced does not directly receive full card numbers or card security codes through its current Stripe Checkout integration.

**Communications:** Support, feedback, waitlist and join submissions, account-deletion requests, and transactional email outbox records. Our email provider tells us whether an email we send was delivered, opened, or clicked, using a tracking pixel and redirected links. We use this only to make sure our emails reach students and are useful.

**Technical:** Servers and service providers may process IP addresses, device/browser details, request times, security events, and error information.

## 3. Uses
We use information to maintain accounts, provide and personalize educational services, manage mock tests, process payments, send necessary service messages, respond to support and privacy requests, protect security, troubleshoot, and meet legal obligations. Aced is not currently sending promotional email campaigns during Public Alpha.

## 4. AI Services
**Groq:** Live student-specific tutor requests are sent to Groq's synchronous inference API. Requests can contain student-written messages and educational context. Aced does not intentionally add student names, emails, account IDs, authentication tokens, or IP addresses to the request body, although students may include personal information in their own messages. Aced has turned on Groq's Zero Data Retention setting for its inference API, so Groq does not store tutor inputs or outputs. This setting does not prevent Groq from processing the request to answer it, and Groq may still keep basic usage records.

**Google Gemini:** Gemini is used separately for educational question generation and review. The deployed interactive tutor does not send student-specific tutor requests to Gemini, including as a fallback. Gemini's free-tier terms may allow submitted educational content to be used for product improvement or human review. Aced does not intentionally use student tutor messages or learning histories in that content-generation workflow.

## 5. Service Providers
Aced uses Google for sign-in, Groq for tutoring, Google Gemini for educational content generation, Stripe for payments, Resend for email (including delivery, open, and click information), Sentry for error monitoring, Amazon Web Services for hosting, databases, and encrypted backups, and Zoom for live lessons. Providers process information under their own terms and controls. Aced does not use advertising or third-party analytics trackers and does not sell student personal information.

## 6. Cookies and Browser Storage
Aced uses authentication cookies and browser storage for preferences, mock-test session continuity, temporary answer recovery, and interface settings. Specifically, Aced uses: a sign-in session cookie; your light/dark theme choice; whether you hid a dashboard card; your place in the first-time tour; and temporary backups of mock-test answers so they survive a refresh. Aced does not use advertising cookies. Clearing browser storage may affect preferences or recovery.

## 7. Retention
We retain data as reasonably needed to provide services, maintain security, manage transactions, and comply with law. Active account and identifiable learning data generally remain while needed for the account, subject to valid deletion requests. Minimal payment, refund, security, and consent records may be retained for legitimate obligations, subject to applicable limits. Backup copies may persist until established rotation removes them. Account data is kept until the account is deleted. Database backups are encrypted and automatically deleted after 30 days.

## 8. Deletion and Privacy Requests
Request deletion through Account & Privacy or support@aceditprep.com. We aim to acknowledge requests within **seven calendar days**. This is not a guarantee that deletion will be completed in seven days. Requests begin a review that may include ownership verification and assessment of legal, payment, or security retention exceptions. Requests do not instantly erase data. Provider-held data and backups may follow separate retention processes. Approved deletions are completed by hand, and we will email you when yours is done.

## 9. De-identified Information
Where permitted by law, Aced may retain genuinely de-identified or aggregated educational information to improve the service. To de-identify information, we remove names, email addresses, and account identifiers and keep only answer-level or aggregated statistics that are not reasonably linkable to you. Removing a name alone does not necessarily de-identify a record.

## 10. Privacy Rights
Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, or deletion of information and learn about our processing practices. Contact support@aceditprep.com. We may verify identity before acting. Email preferences do not prevent necessary transactional communications.

## 11. Security
We use access controls and other technical safeguards, but no online system can guarantee absolute security. Report concerns to support@aceditprep.com.

## 12. Processing Locations
Aced primarily serves users in the United States. Some providers may process data elsewhere under their infrastructure and agreements. We do not guarantee that all information remains in one state or country.

## 13. Updates
We may update this Policy and will provide notice where required by law.
