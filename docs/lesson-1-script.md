# Lesson 1 · Your first smart move

## Part 1 · Start calm
[BOARD: Read the question before the choices.]
ASTRA SAYS: Before you solve anything, take one calm breath and read what the question is actually asking.

## Part 2 · Find the job
[BOARD: Circle the task word: best, main, or most likely.]
ASTRA SAYS: The task word tells you the job. It keeps you from doing extra work.

## Part 3 · Use evidence
[BOARD: Point to one piece of evidence.]
ASTRA SAYS: Great answers have proof. Point to one fact, number, or sentence that supports your choice.

## Part 4 · Rule out noise
[BOARD: Cross out choices that answer a different question.]
ASTRA SAYS: If an answer sounds smart but answers a different question, cross it out and keep moving.

## Part 5 · Try it
[BOARD: 1. Read the task word.]
ASTRA SAYS: Let’s try one together. Read the task word first, because it tells you exactly what your answer needs to do.
[BOARD: 2. Find one piece of proof.]
ASTRA SAYS: Next, find one piece of proof. You do not need every detail. You need the detail that answers the question.
[CHAT 10s]
[BOARD: 3. Choose the answer that matches both.]
ASTRA SAYS: Now choose the answer that matches the task word and your proof. That is your Aced move: clear, calm, and evidence first.
ASTRA SAYS: Keep practicing this pattern, and the ACT starts to feel less like guessing and more like noticing.
